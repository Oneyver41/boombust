Boom & Bust Pipeline

How companies rise. Why they fall.

Boom & Bust Pipeline is a private production tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Boom & Bust Files. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Uploads videos produced by the channel owner to their own YouTube channel as private, together with thumbnails and subtitles, so the owner can review and publish them manually.
  • Reads the channel's own statistics and analytics to improve future videos.
  • Copies review files to folders in the owner's Google Drive that the app itself created.

Google user data

The app only accesses the account of the channel owner who authorized it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
